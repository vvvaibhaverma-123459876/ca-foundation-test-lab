(i) 2 marks — objectives: checking arithmetical accuracy of ledger postings, basis for final accounts, locating errors; errors not disclosed: errors of omission (complete), errors of principle, compensating errors, errors of original entry, wrong posting to correct side of a wrong account. (ii) 2 marks — Materiality: items significant enough to influence decisions are disclosed separately (e.g., small stationery items charged to expense rather than capitalised). Prudence: anticipate no profit but provide for all known liabilities/losses (e.g., provision for doubtful debts, inventory at lower of cost or NRV). Substance over form: record economic reality (e.g., a hire-purchase/finance lease asset shown in the buyer's/lessee's books though legal title has not passed). (iii) 2 marks — Provision is a charge against profit for a known liability whose amount cannot be determined with substantial accuracy; created even if there is a loss; e.g., provision for depreciation/doubtful debts. Reserve is an appropriation of profit to strengthen financial position, created only out of profits; e.g., general reserve; reserves form part of shareholders' funds while provisions are liabilities/asset deductions.
